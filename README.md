My philosophy on life and education is that the success of learning lies entirely in the hands of the individual and their effort in being willing to understand how they view and understand the world. I believe we are all both teachers and students alike, constantly learning from each other in both direct and indirect ways. As educators, it is our duty to cultivate a deep and personal relationship with our own learning. That means pushing our boundaries and being open and receptive towards our students and their beliefs. As students, we must commit to our education, explore foundational concepts, and use these insights to discover and understand ourselves. When learning you must persistenetly question and challenege your beliefs and strive to create new meanings through your experiences. This lifelong journey is about constant improvement through learning, adapting, and evolving. Together and separately, let’s create new meaning not only for ourselves but for others as well by being ourselves and discovering what the world means to us.
